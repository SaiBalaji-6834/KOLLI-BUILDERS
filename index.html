GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before
we get started?

Here are a few useful starting points. We can customise these answers once the company's actual process is confirmed.

What information should I share to get an estimate?+

Share the project type, approximate location, plot or floor area if known, desired scope, budget range and target timeline. Drawings and reference photos are helpful but not essential for an initial conversation.

Do you handle both homes and commercial buildings?+

This website is planned for a company serving both residential and commercial customers. Confirm the exact services offered before publishing.

Can I discuss a renovation project?+

Yes, if renovation is part of the company's service offering. Describe the existing space, the changes you want and any known site constraints.

How long will my project take?+

Timing depends on project size, design, approvals, site conditions, material availability and scope. A realistic schedule should be discussed after reviewing the project details.
